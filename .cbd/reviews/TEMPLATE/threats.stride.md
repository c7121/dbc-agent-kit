# Threat model — STRIDE

Use the DFD to enumerate threats.

## Threats
Each threat:
- id: STRIDE-001
- DFD element: (process/data flow/data store/external entity)
- category: S | T | R | I | D | E
- description
- impacted assets
- likelihood: low/med/high
- impact: low/med/high
- mitigation(s)
- verification (test/monitoring/control)
- status: open | mitigated | accepted-risk | deferred

## Notes
- Keep mitigations concrete and actionable.
- Prefer mitigations that can be turned into `.cbd/tasks/*`.
